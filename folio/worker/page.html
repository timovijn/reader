Some websites or browsers block bookmarklets. You can still copy the article’s text and use “paste article text”. Nothing is sent from the source page to Folio automatically.

THE IDEA BEHIND FOLIO

The internet, at a slower pace.

Folio gives good writing a little breathing room. Paste a link, settle into a clean reading view, and make the page feel like your own.

Article extraction works best on publicly accessible, text-based pages. Paywalls, sign-in screens, bot protection and pages that load their text with JavaScript may prevent extraction. Folio does not bypass these restrictions.

Your collection and reading preferences stay in this browser. Saved articles don’t sync across devices, and clearing browser data removes them. Opening a link fetches that page through Folio’s server; article images are excluded. The sample essays are original Folio demonstrations.
