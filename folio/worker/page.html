THE IDEA BEHIND OTHER LIVES

A way into other lives.

Through reading, we experience lives beyond our own: different places, different perspectives, different ways of being. This is a quiet place to spend time with those stories.

Article extraction works best on publicly accessible, text-based pages. Paywalls, sign-in screens, bot protection and pages that load their text with JavaScript may prevent extraction. other lives does not bypass these restrictions.

Your collection and reading preferences stay in this browser. Saved articles don’t sync across devices, and clearing browser data removes them. Opening a link fetches that page through other lives’s server; article images are excluded.
